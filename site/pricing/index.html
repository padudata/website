Questions

How pricing works

Why isn't there a price on the page?
The work depends on how many systems you run and how many records they hold. A company with one ERP and 800 vendors needs far less than a group with three ERPs and 20,000. We send a fixed quote after a short call, so you know the full cost before anything starts.
What do you need from us for a Master Data Review?
Exports of your vendor and customer master from each system, and an hour with the people who maintain them. We agree on how the data is shared and handled before you send anything.
Is the free health check really free?
Yes. It runs entirely in your browser and has no usage limit. Nothing you load into it is sent to us.
When will the platform be available?
It is in development. Join the early access list and we'll contact you when it is ready for your systems.
How do we pay?
By bank transfer against an invoice, in Indonesian rupiah.
